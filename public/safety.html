Playing safely

For players, parents and guardians · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Bazaar is for ages 13 and up. Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before you play online.

Tips for players

  • Use a made-up nickname, not your real name.
  • Never share your address, school, phone number, social media, photos or passwords, even with someone friendly.
  • Play online rooms with people you know. Share invite links only with friends.
  • If anyone makes you uncomfortable, mute them, leave the room, and tell an adult you trust.
  • Nobody from Bazaar will ever ask you for personal details, money or passwords.

Safety tools in the game

  • Mute anyone: tap a player's name in chat to hide their messages and silence their voice, just for you.
  • No contact details: links, email addresses, phone numbers and social media handles are replaced with [hidden] in chat, and can't be used as nicknames.
  • Swear filter: common swear words in English and Hindi are hidden in chat, and blocked in nicknames.
  • Voice is optional: your microphone is used only after you press Join voice. You can mute yourself (press M) or leave the call at any time.
  • Private rooms: rooms can only be joined with their long random code or invite link. There's no public list of rooms or players.
  • Hosts: the person who made the room can remove players before the game starts, and a removed player can't come back into that room.
  • Nothing kept: no accounts, profiles or friend lists, and chat disappears when the room is deleted. See the Privacy Policy.

For parents and guardians

  • Bazaar is a free board game with pretend money. It has no ads, purchases, betting or prizes.
  • Online play includes text chat and optional voice chat with the other people in the room. For younger players, we suggest playing with family and friends you know, or against the computer bots, which need no chat at all.
  • You can turn off microphone access for the site in your browser settings to stop voice chat completely.
  • If you think your child has shared personal information, or someone behaved inappropriately, contact us (below) and we'll help.

Getting help

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services straight away. In India:

  • Emergency: 112
  • Childline (children in need of help): 1098
  • Cyber crime helpline: 1930, or report online at cybercrime.gov.in

Contact us

To report a problem with the game, open an issue on the game's GitHub page. It is public, so don't include personal details. Just ask to talk privately. Security problems can be reported privately as described in security.txt.
