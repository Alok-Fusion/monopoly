Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, age, location or payment details.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game sets no cookies.
  • A game room exists only in the game server's memory while people are playing. It is deleted soon after everyone leaves and is never saved to a database.
  • Voice chat is optional. Your microphone is used only after you press Join voice, and calls are never recorded.

Who we are

Bazaar is a free browser game made by Alok Kushwaha, who is responsible for the personal data described here (the "data fiduciary" or "controller"). To contact us, see Contact below.

What we handle, and why

What Why How long
The nickname, token and colour you pick, and a random player ID To show you on the board and in the room Until the room is deleted (see below)
Your game moves, trades and bids To play the game Until the room is deleted
Chat messages (the last 100 in the room) To show them to the other players Until the room is deleted
Your IP address and basic connection details To connect you, to stop abuse (for example, too many connections at once), and to keep anyone the host removed out of that room Held in the game server's memory while you are connected and for a few minutes after (for a removed player, until the room is deleted); never logged or saved by us
Your voice, if you join voice chat To let players in the call hear you Not stored at all: it is sent live and never recorded

When a room is deleted: 10 minutes after the last person leaves if the game never started, 2 hours after the last person leaves if it did (so players can come back after a dropped connection), or whenever the game server restarts. Nothing about a room is kept after that.

Our legal basis: we process this information because you ask us to, by choosing to play (your consent and the performance of the service you requested), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and free from abuse.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's own storage so it remembers you next time. They never leave your device unless the game needs them to reconnect you. They are not cookies and are not used for tracking.

  • Your nickname, token and colour, and whether sound is on (richup_player_name, richup_player_color, richup_token_id, richup_sound_enabled)
  • A single-player game you can continue later (bazaar_saved_game)
  • Your seat in the last online room and its secret key, so you can rejoin after a refresh, for up to a day (bazaar_last_seat)
  • For this browser tab only: your player ID and whether you have seen the welcome screen (dt_player_id, bazaar_cover_seen)

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Who else is involved

We don't sell or rent anyone's data, and we don't share it with anyone except these services, which are needed to run the game:

  • Other players in your room see your nickname, token, colour, moves and chat messages.
  • Voice chat partners: voice goes directly between players' browsers, or through a relay service (Metered) when a direct link isn't possible. Audio is encrypted in transit. As with any direct call, the other people in a voice call can technically see your IP address. If that worries you, don't join voice, or only join with people you know.
  • PeerJS runs a free matchmaking server that helps browsers in a voice call find each other. It sees your IP address and a random voice ID.
  • Vercel hosts the website, and Render hosts the game server. Like any web host, they may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Unsplash serves the landmark photos. Your browser downloads them directly, so Unsplash sees your IP address. The game's fonts come from our own site.

Some of these services are outside India, so your data may be handled in other countries, under those companies' own privacy commitments.

Children

Bazaar is meant for players aged 13 and over. Anyone under 18 should play only with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game never asks for a real name or contact details, and we suggest children use a made-up nickname and play with people they know.

To protect younger players, chat hides links, email addresses, phone numbers and social media handles, filters swear words and reminds players not to share personal details. Any player can be muted, the host can remove someone from the room for good, and voice chat only starts when someone chooses to join it. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has shared personal information in the game, contact us and we will help remove it. See our Safety page for more.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or the GDPR in Europe), you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent, or complain. Because rooms are short-lived and we hold no accounts, there is usually nothing left to delete once you leave. You can also simply clear this site's data from your browser. To make a request or raise a complaint, see Contact. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Data Protection Board of India.

How we protect it

  • All connections use HTTPS and secure WebSockets (encrypted in transit).
  • The game server checks every move against the rules, so a modified browser can't cheat or act for another player.
  • Each seat has a secret key, so nobody can take over another player's seat.
  • Team-only chat is sent only to that team, and chat hides contact details and links.
  • Room codes are long and random, so rooms can't be found by guessing.
  • The server limits messages, chat speed and connections to stop spam and abuse, and the website sends strict security headers.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security problem, please report it privately, as described in security.txt.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. If you keep playing after a change, the new policy applies.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: open an issue on the game's GitHub page. Issues there are public, so please don't include personal details. Just say you'd like to talk privately and we'll arrange it.
